## 2. Research: what matters about The Park

**Company and brand**
- Founded 1967 with The Park Kolkata on Park Street. The group's name comes from the street. Chairperson Priya Paul (since about 1990) began the design repositioning in 1992. THE Park Bangalore (2000) was designed by Terence Conran, and six hotels are long-time Design Hotels members.
- "Anything But Ordinary" has been the tagline since the 2013 rebrand.
- Five brands: **THE Park**, **THE Park Collection**, **Zone by The Park**, **Zone Connect by The Park**, **Stop by Zone**. The company calls itself India's 8th largest hotel chain. The group also owns **Flurys**.
- About 42 hotels in 32 cities (Business Traveller, Oct 2026), with a target of about **85 hotels by FY2030**. Priya Paul says five THE Park hotels are under construction. Zone is the fast-scaling, partner-developed brand.

**Performance (Q1 FY27, quarter to 30 June 2026)**
- Revenue from operations Rs 166.8 crore, up 8.1% year on year. PAT Rs 11.5 crore, down 14.2%.
- Occupancy **92%**. RevPAR about Rs 6,858. Sources disagree on ARR (Rs 7,459 vs Rs 9,310); Rs 7,459 is the figure consistent with RevPAR.
- Food, beverage, nightlife and lifestyle dining were about **43% of revenue** (IDBI Capital review).
- Management guides to high single-digit ARR growth.

**Growth and pipeline**
- March 2026: Zone by The Park Darjeeling, Zone Connect Gangtok and Zone Connect Katra opened. Also Zone Connect Patna (Jan 2026).
- Signed: Zone Connect Ayodhya (56 rooms) and Ujjain (64 rooms); Zone Connect Bharatpur (63 keys, HMA signed Oct 2026); THE Park in a Siliguri tea estate (100 rooms); THE Park Juhu, Mumbai (mid-2027); The Park Unizen with Ambuja Neotia (Feb 2026).
- **Ran Baas The Palace, Patiala** (THE Park Collection) opened January 2025: 35 suites, Punjab's first luxury palace hotel, named among the world's most beautiful hotels of 2025 by Prix Versailles.
- **Flurys**: 111 stores (Q1 FY27). First standalone North India café, 24 hours, in Gurugram (Aug 2026). Targeting 130 to 140 outlets by FY27 and about 300 over the medium term. **Centenary in 2027.**

**Venues (the cultural assets)**
- Kolkata: Someplace Else (the city's long-running live-music pub), Tantra (two-floor nightclub), Roxy, Zen, Aqua.
- New Delhi (Connaught Place): Aqua (poolside), Fire, Agni. The dining concept follows the five elements.
- Hyderabad: Aish.
- Group-run culture in Kolkata: the Park Street Christmas festival (co-organised by the Apeejay Surrendra Group) and the Apeejay Kolkata Literary Festival (17th edition, January 2026).

**Social and audience context**
- Last public snapshots found: @theparkhotels about 28K Instagram followers (about 2024), Facebook about 113K, LinkedIn about 84K. **Check the live numbers before quoting any of these.**
- Booking.com, via Outlook Traveller: 80% of Indian travellers plan to spend most of their time at the property, against 51% globally.
- Younger Indians discover travel on Reels and book elsewhere, and they increasingly use Instagram as a search engine. Both points come from industry blogs, so treat them as directional.

## 3. The strategy, in short

1. **The business problem:** the rooms are full (92%), so growth comes from rate, covers (43% of revenue) and about 40 new openings. All three depend on desire.
2. **The insight:** The Park was named after Park Street, Calcutta's after-dark mile of jazz, cabaret and late dinners. It started as an address with a scene around it.
3. **The idea:** *Park Street, everywhere.* Every THE Park is its city's Park Street. "Anything but ordinary" stays as the sign-off.
4. **Four collections:** After Hours, By Design, The Table, The City.
5. **Six ideas:** The Sessions (Someplace Else), The 3 a.m. Menu, Room 1967, Night Shift, Don't Check Out, Flurys 100.
6. **Timing:** Diwali, then Christmas on Park Street, New Year's Eve, AKLF, and in 2027 Flurys at 100 and Juhu.
7. **From scroll to seat:** Discover, Desire, Ask (WhatsApp), Book (direct, tracked).
8. **Measurement:** share of voice and saves (rate), tables booked from social (covers), direct room nights, and enquiries in each new hotel's first 90 days.
