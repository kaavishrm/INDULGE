Mumbai's new Friday ritual is a shop window

Westside numbers one look a week, in every one of its 311 windows. Members see it on Thursday night.

By Saturday afternoon the rail inside the D.N. Nagar door is half empty, and the store's WhatsApp has a list of holds waiting for Sunday. Window No. 047 is a blazer and trousers called 'Office, Monday'. The format is simple, and that is the point: any of the 311 store teams can dress it on a Friday morning.
